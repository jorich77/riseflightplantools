BONUS 1
Clear for Take-Off

Your strategy is only as strong as the belief running underneath it. This bonus covers the inner work - the fears, stories, and patterns that either power your progress or quietly sabotage it. If you've tried to build a business before and stopped, or if the fear of being visible is louder than your ambition right now, start here.

What's covered
  • Why most people self-sabotage before they even begin - and how to spot it in yourself
  • The beliefs that keep smart, capable women stuck - and the reframes that shift them
  • How to tell the difference between a strategy problem and a mindset problem
  • What to do when imposter syndrome, fear of visibility, or perfectionism show up
  • The ATM framework - Answer, Tools, Mentoring - for working through any block

Time Management for Busy Mums

The number one reason people don't finish programmes is not motivation - it's not protecting their time. This bonus is for anyone who is wondering how they'll fit this in around work, kids, and everything else life throws at them. The answer is not "find more time". It's use the time you have differently.

What's covered
  • Why "I don't have time" is never the real problem - and what is
  • How to build a business in 30-minute windows - the micro-session method
  • The weekly planning system that keeps you moving without burning out
  • How to protect your business time without guilt - the non-negotiable session
  • What to do on the days when life takes over completely
