What this step is about

Before you build anything, you need to know two things: where you want to go, and where you actually are right now. Step 1 gives you both. Start with the Freedom Vision Questionnaire to get crystal clear on what your freedom life looks like - then complete your Freedom Score Scorecard to honestly rate where you are today across five key life areas. The gap between those two things is what this programme is here to close.

In this step you will
  • Complete your Freedom Score Scorecard across 5 life areas
  • Record your "before" - your income, hours, and what's draining you
  • Get a personalised insight based on your lowest scoring areas
  • Set your starting benchmark to measure your growth against
