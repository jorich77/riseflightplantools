What this step is about

Every external strategy you have is only as strong as the mindset running underneath it. Step 7 is the inner work - the beliefs, fears, and stories that either power your progress or quietly sabotage it. This step doesn't have a tool. It has a conversation. Work through the questions in this module honestly and bring what comes up to your next coaching session.

In this step you will
  • Identify the core belief that is most holding you back right now
  • Understand the difference between a strategy problem and a mindset problem
  • Work through the ATM framework: Answer (the reframe), Tools, Mentoring
  • Bring your honest reflection to your next coaching call
