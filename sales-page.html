This is exactly what Step 2 is for. The Business Path Decision Framework walks you through six proven online business models and helps you identify the best fit for your skills, time, and income goals. You don't need to know what you want to do before you start.

The programme is built with mums in mind - around school runs, nap times, and the reality of building something while life is already full. But the framework works for anyone who wants to build an online business around a busy life.
