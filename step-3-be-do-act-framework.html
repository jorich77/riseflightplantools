Be, Do, Act

Name your 3 biggest blocks. Work through the framework. Find the one small step you'll take every day.

Be
Identity
Who do you want to be? Decide her first.
Do
Action
What would she do? Do that today.
Act
Daily step
One small action. Every day. Non-negotiable.
