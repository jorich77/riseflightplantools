AI HACK
Write your bio in 60 seconds
Copy this prompt to get three bio options you can test.
I need to write an Instagram/Facebook bio. Here are my details: Who I help: [your audience] What I help them do: [the result] My credibility in one line: [why you're the right person] My call to action: [what I want them to do next - book a call, DM me, click the link] Please write me 3 bio options: 1. Clear and direct - states exactly what I do 2. Story-led - opens with a relatable hook about my audience's problem 3. Bold and punchy - makes someone stop scrolling Each must be under 150 characters. Use sentence case, not ALL CAPS. End each with a simple CTA.

An email list is your most valuable business asset. Social media followers are rented - your email list is owned. Start building it from day one with these free tools.

Mailerlite
Free up to 1,000
The cleanest, simplest email marketing tool for beginners. Free up to 1,000 subscribers and 12,000 emails per month. Includes landing pages, automation, and forms - everything you need to start building your list.
Best for: Email list building, simple automations, lead magnet delivery
mailerlite.com →
Brevo (formerly Sendinblue)
Free plan
Free plan includes unlimited contacts and up to 300 emails per day. Slightly more powerful than Mailerlite for automations. A great free option as your list grows.
Best for: Email list, automations, transactional emails
brevo.com →
Google Forms
Free
The fastest way to start capturing leads before you set up a proper email tool. Create a simple form, share the link, collect responses in Google Sheets. Not fancy but it works instantly.
Best for: Quick lead capture, waitlist sign-ups, discovery call applications
forms.google.com →
WhatsApp Communities
Free
Build a free community directly in WhatsApp. Great for nurturing warm leads, sharing updates, and building relationships at a more personal level than email. Incredibly high open rates.
Best for: Warm community building, high-engagement audience nurturing
whatsapp.com →
AI HACK
Write your lead magnet and welcome email
Use this prompt to create your free opt-in offer and the first email new subscribers receive.
I am building an email list and I need help with two things. My niche: I help [who you help] who [their problem] to [the result]. My business model: [coaching / digital products / services / etc.] My offer: [what you sell] Please write me: 1. Three lead magnet ideas that would attract my ideal client and that I could create quickly (checklist, guide, template, mini training, etc.) 2. A subject line and welcome email for whichever lead magnet I choose - warm, genuine, and not salesy 3. A social media post promoting my lead magnet to drive sign-ups For the welcome email: - Tell them what to expect from me - Give them the lead magnet or tell them how to access it - End with a soft next step (not a hard sell) - Tone: like a message from a friend who's good at this stuff

You need to be able to receive money before you have a client - not after. Set one of these up today. It takes minutes.

PayPal.me
Free to set up
The fastest payment link to set up. PayPal.me/yourname gives you a direct payment link you can send anywhere. Small transaction fee per payment. Works globally. Perfect for your first client.
Best for: First client payments, quick invoicing, international payments
paypal.me →
Stripe
Free to set up
More professional than PayPal - creates branded payment pages and handles subscriptions, payment plans, and one-off payments. Small % fee per transaction. Takes a few days to set up and verify your account.
Best for: Professional checkout pages, payment plans, recurring payments
stripe.com →
Systeme.io
Free plan
An all-in-one platform that combines checkout, email marketing, courses, and sales funnels. The free plan is remarkably generous. If you want everything in one place from the start, this is it.
Best for: All-in-one checkout + email + course hosting
systeme.io →
Buy Me a Coffee
Free to set up
Simple platform for one-off payments, memberships, and digital product sales. Very easy to set up and looks clean. Good for coaches and creators who want a simple, no-fuss payment option.
Best for: Simple one-off payments, digital downloads, memberships
buymeacoffee.com →
AI HACK
Write your payment confirmation message
The message someone gets right after they pay sets the tone for the whole relationship. Use this prompt to write it.
I've just set up my payment system and I need to write the message a client receives immediately after they pay. Here are my details: My offer: [what they've just bought] What happens next: [what you'll do now they've paid - e.g. you'll send them a welcome email, schedule a call, give them access] The tone I want to set: [warm and professional / exciting and personal / calm and reassuring] My name: [your name] Please write me: 1. A payment confirmation message (short, warm, reassuring - confirms they made the right decision) 2. A first follow-up message or email to send within the hour (what to expect, what to do next, how excited you are to work with them) Make both feel genuinely personal - not automated or corporate.

AI HACK
Plan your first month of content
Use this prompt to generate a full month of content ideas tailored to your niche.
I need to plan my content for the next 30 days. Here are my details: My niche: I help [who you help] who [their problem] to [the result]. My platforms: [Instagram / Facebook / TikTok / LinkedIn] How often I can post: [e.g. 5x per week] My 3 content pillars: [e.g. 1. My story and journey 2. Tips and education 3. My offer and results] My business model: [coaching / digital products / services / network marketing / high-ticket] Please create: 1. A 30-day content plan with one post idea per day, rotating across my 3 pillars 2. The format for each post (reel, carousel, talking head, text post, story) 3. A hook for each post (the first line that makes someone stop scrolling) Make the ideas specific to my niche - not generic. Use language my ideal client would recognise immediately.

Free
Google Docs, Sheets, Drive, and Calendar - all free and all you need for basic business organisation. Use Docs for client materials, Sheets for tracking, Drive for storage, and Calendar for scheduling.

Free plan
Share a booking link and let people schedule calls with you automatically - no back-and-forth messaging to find a time. The free plan allows one meeting type (e.g. a 30-minute discovery call). Essential from day one.

Free plan

Free

Wave
Free

AI HACK
Build your client onboarding system
Use this prompt to create everything you need to professionally onboard a new client.
I've just signed my first client and I want to onboard them professionally. Here are my details: My offer: [what they've bought - e.g. 8-week coaching programme] What's included: [list the main deliverables] How we'll communicate: [e.g. weekly Zoom calls, WhatsApp support, email] Tools they'll need to access: [e.g. Zoom, WhatsApp, any resources] My name and contact: [your name and how they reach you] Please create: 1. A welcome email to send within the hour of them signing up (warm, excited, clear on next steps) 2. A client onboarding checklist - what I need from them and what they need to know before we start 3. A short "how this works" overview I can share with them (3-4 paragraphs, sets expectations, tone of the relationship) Make it feel personal and professional - not templated. They should feel excited and confident they made the right decision.
