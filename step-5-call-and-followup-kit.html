A 30-minute discovery call has five phases. Stick to the timing - it keeps you in control and shows professionalism.

0-3 minWelcome and set the frame
→
Thank them for their time. Tell them how the call will work: "I'll ask you a few questions to understand where you are, you can ask me anything, and at the end we'll both know whether it makes sense to work together."
This removes pressure immediately. They're not being sold to - they're being heard.
3-12 minUnderstand their situation
Q
Tell me about where you are right now - what does your day-to-day look like?
Q
What made you say yes to this call today?
Q
How long has this been a problem for you?
Q
What have you already tried? What happened?
Listen more than you talk here. Take notes.
12-20 minExplore where they want to go
Q
If we fast forward 12 months and things have gone really well - what does that look like for you?
Q
What would change in your life if you hit that goal?
Q
What's the cost of staying where you are?
This is where they sell themselves. Your job is to help them articulate it.
20-27 minPresent your solution
→
Only now do you share what you offer. Connect it directly to what they told you: "Based on what you've shared, here's how I think I can help..."
Keep it brief. Cover: what it is, what's included, the result they can expect, and the investment.
27-30 minClose and next steps
Q
How does that sound to you?
Q
Do you have any questions before we talk about next steps?
→
If they're a yes: tell them exactly what happens next (payment link, onboarding, start date).
If they need to think: agree a specific follow-up time. Never leave it as "I'll be in touch."

Five minutes of preparation makes the difference between a call that flows and one that feels awkward. Run through this before every call.

Before the call
Look at their social profile - note one specific thing to reference
Re-read your DM conversation so the context is fresh
Have your offer details ready - price, what's included, payment link
Have this call outline open in front of you
Note-taking ready (pen and paper or open doc)
Quiet space, good signal, headphones in
Mindset check
I am here to find out if I can genuinely help this person - not to close at all costs
A no is fine - not everyone is the right fit
I will ask more than I say in the first half of this call
I believe in what I offer and what it can do for the right person
After the call
Send a follow-up message within 2 hours (use your template)
Note down what they shared - for future conversations and content ideas
Log the outcome in your weekly stats tracker

Most sales happen in the follow-up. Most people give up after one message. Send all three of these - spaced out, warm, and never desperate.

Message 1 - Same day or next morning Within 24 hours
Hey [name], really enjoyed our conversation today. What you shared about [specific thing they mentioned] really stayed with me - it's exactly the kind of situation I love working with. I've sent the details over - have a read when you get a chance and let me know if you have any questions. No rush at all.

Message 2 - Value add 3-4 days later
Hi [name], just thinking of you after our call. I came across [a resource / a post / something relevant] that made me think of what you mentioned about [their specific problem] - thought it might be useful. [Share the thing.] Still happy to answer any questions about working together whenever you're ready.

Message 3 - Gentle close 7-10 days later
Hey [name], just circling back one last time - I know life gets busy. I have [a spot opening up / a new cohort starting on X date] and wanted to make sure you had the chance to grab it if it still feels right. If the timing isn't there, completely understand - just let me know either way so I can plan accordingly. Hope you're doing well.

After message 3: if there's still no response, move on. You've shown up three times with warmth and zero pressure. Anyone who is meant to work with you will respond. Keep creating content - they may come back in their own time.
