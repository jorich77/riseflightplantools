The hook is the first line of your content. It decides whether someone stops scrolling or moves on. These 20 proven formulas work across reels, carousels, and captions. Copy any hook and fill in the brackets.

Pain point hooks
If you're [doing X] and still not getting [result], this is why.
Nobody talks about how hard it is to [specific struggle]. So I will.
The reason you're still [stuck situation] isn't what you think.
Stop [common mistake]. Do this instead.
Curiosity hooks
I used to think [wrong belief]. Then this happened.
Here's what no one tells you about [topic].
The [number] things I wish I knew before [starting X].
This one shift changed everything for me. And it took less than a week.
Story hooks
A year ago I was [where you were]. Today I'm [where you are now].
I almost gave up on [thing]. Here's what stopped me.
The moment I realised [realisation], everything changed.
I was [relatable situation] when I finally decided enough was enough.
Bold statement hooks
You don't need [common belief] to [achieve result]. Here's proof.
[Controversial take]. And I'll explain why.
Most [your niche audience] get this completely wrong.
The [thing everyone tells you to do] is keeping you stuck.
Offer and result hooks
My client went from [before] to [after] in [timeframe]. Here's how.
What would your life look like if [dream outcome]? Let me show you how to get there.
I help [your audience] do [result]. And I'm looking for my next [number] clients.
If you've been waiting for a sign to start [thing] - this is it.

Bold statement hooks - a take that makes them stop and react 5. Offer/result hooks - attract people who are ready to buy For each hook: - Fill in the brackets with specifics from my niche (don't leave placeholders) - Keep it under 15 words where possible - Make it feel like a real person wrote it, not a template I want to copy and paste these directly into content - no editing required.

Stories build trust faster than any other format. Use these 7 prompts - one per day - and rotate the cycle each week. You never need to think about what to post in your stories again.

AI HACK
Write your story captions for the week
Copy this prompt to get 7 ready-to-post story captions tailored to your niche.
I need 7 Instagram/Facebook story captions for this week - one per day. My niche: I help [who you help] who [their problem] to [the result]. My story / background: [one sentence about your journey - where you were, where you are now] My offer: [what you sell] My personality / tone: [warm and funny / honest and direct / educational / motivational] Please write one caption for each of these 7 story types: Day 1 - Behind the scenes (show my real day) Day 2 - Education (one tip for my audience) Day 3 - Personal share (something vulnerable or honest) Day 4 - Poll or question (get my audience talking) Day 5 - Client win or testimonial (share a result) Day 6 - My offer (promote without being pushy) Day 7 - Motivation (end the week with something they need to hear) For each caption: - Keep it under 3 lines (stories are short) - Add a CTA where relevant (reply, DM, swipe up, book a call) - Make it feel like a real message, not a post
