Warm DM Outreach
The fastest route to your first client. No ads, no funnel - just conversations.
Time to first lead
24-48 hours
Investment needed
Zero
Best for
Getting first clients fast
Effort level
Daily, 20-30 minutes

What it is

Warm DM outreach means proactively starting conversations with people who already have some awareness of you - they follow you, liked your post, watched your reel, or fit your ideal client profile. You're not cold calling strangers. You're opening a door that's already slightly ajar.

Who to reach out to

  • People who liked or commented on your last 5-10 posts
  • People who watched more than 50% of your reels (check your insights)
  • New followers who match your ideal client profile
  • People who replied to your stories
  • People who viewed your profile multiple times
  • Existing contacts in your phone who fit your ideal client

How to do it - the daily routine

  • Spend 10 minutes engaging genuinely on their content before you DM - like posts, leave a real comment
  • Send 5-10 DMs per day - not a pitch, a conversation opener based on something specific about them
  • Use the DM framework from your Sales Conversation Playbook to move from conversation to booked call
  • Track every conversation in a simple spreadsheet: name, date contacted, status, next step
  • Follow up with anyone who went quiet using your Follow-Up Kit
Important: never open with a pitch. Comment on something real - their content, their bio, something they shared. People can smell copy-paste. Personalise every single message.
Your week 1 actions
Start here
  • Make a list of 20 people to reach out to this week
  • Send 5 warm DMs today - conversation openers only, no pitch
  • Set up a simple tracker (Google Sheet: name, date, status, next step)
  • Aim for 3 conversations booked as discovery calls by end of week

What it is

A lead magnet is a free resource you give away in exchange for someone's email address. Once you have their email, you nurture them with a sequence of emails that builds trust and naturally leads into your paid offer. Your email list is the one platform you own - no algorithm can take it away.

What makes a great lead magnet

  • Solves one specific problem your ideal client has right now
  • Delivers a quick win - they should feel better or clearer within minutes of consuming it
  • Is simple to create - a checklist, a template, a mini guide, a quiz, a short video training
  • Naturally leads into your paid offer - it solves a small problem that points to the bigger one you solve
  • Has a compelling name that makes someone immediately think "I need that"

How to set it up

  • Create your lead magnet (aim for something you can build in a day - a PDF, a Canva checklist, a Loom video)
  • Set up a free Mailerlite or Brevo account and create a simple landing page
  • Write a 3-5 email welcome sequence: email 1 delivers the resource, emails 2-5 build trust and introduce your offer
  • Add your lead magnet link to your bio, your stories, and mention it in your content regularly
  • Promote it consistently - at least 3 times a week across your content
The most common mistake: creating a lead magnet and never promoting it. Your lead magnet needs to be mentioned as often as your offer. Make it part of your daily content rotation.
Your week 1 actions
Start here
  • Decide on your lead magnet (what one quick win can you give your ideal client?)
  • Create it this week - done beats perfect
  • Set up Mailerlite free account and create your landing page
  • Write your first 3 welcome emails
  • Post about your lead magnet every day this week

What it is

A challenge is a free 3-7 day event where participants complete a daily action toward a specific goal. A free workshop is a single live session delivering a transformation. Both build your email list, create trust through delivered value, and end with a natural pitch to your paid offer. Challenges in particular are one of the most powerful conversion tools available to online business owners.

How to run a challenge

  • Choose a specific, achievable outcome for your 5-day challenge (e.g. "Find your business idea in 5 days")
  • Create a simple daily action for each day - short, specific, completable in 15-30 minutes
  • Host it in a free Facebook group or on Zoom - keep it simple
  • Promote it for 2 weeks before it starts - aim for 20+ sign-ups before you run it for the first time
  • Deliver daily content (a video, a live, or a post) with the day's task
  • On day 4 or 5, present your paid offer as the natural next step for people who want to go further

How to run a free workshop

  • Pick one problem you can solve in 60-90 minutes with a clear before/after
  • Promote for 1-2 weeks, aim for 10+ sign-ups
  • Deliver the workshop on Zoom (free plan is fine for under 40 minutes)
  • Reserve the last 10-15 minutes to present your paid offer
  • Follow up with everyone who attended within 24 hours

Zero to minimal

First clients + fast trust

Relationship-led, consistent

What it is

Offline and referral strategies use real-world relationships to generate clients - networking events, word of mouth, asking existing clients for referrals, speaking at events, or partnering with complementary businesses. People buy from people they know and trust. Offline methods build that trust faster than any algorithm.

Networking events

  • Search Eventbrite, Meetup, or Facebook Events for local business networking events in your area
  • Attend at least one event per month - consistency builds recognition
  • Have your one-sentence audience statement ready: "I help [who] to [result]"
  • Focus on asking questions and listening - the person who asks the best questions is remembered most
  • Follow up within 24 hours with everyone you spoke to - a LinkedIn connection and a short personal message

Word of mouth + referrals

  • Tell everyone in your network what you do - friends, family, former colleagues, neighbours
  • After completing work with a client, always ask: "Do you know anyone else who might benefit from this?"
  • Create a simple referral incentive: a discount off their next session or a thank-you gift
  • Post about client wins and results publicly (with permission) - this is passive word of mouth
  • Ask satisfied clients to leave a Google review or a testimonial you can share

Speaking and partnerships

  • Offer to speak at local women's groups, business clubs, or community events - even for free at first
  • Partner with complementary businesses who serve the same audience (e.g. a yoga teacher if you help women with wellbeing)
  • Offer to be a guest on local podcasts or online shows in your niche
  • Run a free talk at a local library, community centre, or co-working space
Your week 1 actions
Start here
  • Tell 10 people in your network what you do this week - in person, by text, or on a call
  • Find one networking event near you this month and register
  • Message your most recent 3 clients and ask if they know anyone who could benefit
  • Write your one-sentence audience statement so you're ready when someone asks what you do

Paid Ads
Scale what's already working. Ads amplify - they don't fix.
Time to first lead
48-72 hours once live
Investment needed
£5-£20/day minimum
Best for
Scaling a proven offer
Effort level
Technical setup + daily monitoring

What it is

Paid ads - primarily Facebook and Instagram ads - put your content or lead magnet in front of a targeted cold audience at scale. They can generate leads quickly, but only work when you already have a proven offer, a clear message, and a working funnel. Ads are an accelerant - they make good things grow faster and bad things burn money faster.

When you're ready for ads

  • You've made at least 3-5 organic sales of your offer - it converts when people know about it
  • You have a clear, specific audience you can target in Ads Manager
  • You have a landing page or lead magnet that converts organic traffic (at least 20-30% opt-in rate)
  • You have a budget you're comfortable spending without guaranteeing a return - start with £5-£10/day
  • You understand the basics of the Facebook Ads Manager interface

How to start with ads

  • Start by boosting content that already performed well organically - this is the lowest risk entry point
  • Run a lead generation campaign to your lead magnet - build your list before you try to sell directly
  • Set a daily budget of £5-£10 and run for 7 days before making any changes
  • Test one variable at a time - one audience, one creative, one hook - change one thing per test
  • Track cost per lead - aim for under £2-£3 per email sign-up for a free lead magnet
Important: Do not run ads until you have made organic sales first. If your message isn't converting for free, spending money on it will just lose money faster. Get organic working first - then scale with ads.
Your week 1 actions (if you're ready)
Start here
  • Confirm you have at least 3 organic sales before running ads
  • Set up your Facebook Business Manager and Ads Manager account
  • Install the Meta Pixel on your landing page
  • Choose your first campaign: boosted post or lead magnet campaign
  • Set your daily budget (£5-£10) and a 7-day test period
