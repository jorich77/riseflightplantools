The four numbers
1
Leads generated
How many new people entered your world this week - new followers, email sign-ups, DM conversations started, or people who raised their hand in some way. This is the top of your pipeline.
2
Conversations started
How many genuine two-way conversations did you have with potential clients this week - DMs, replies, comments, emails. Not broadcast, not posting - actual back-and-forth dialogue with a real person.
3
Discovery calls booked
How many calls did you book this week from conversations - either completed this week or booked for next. This is the bridge between conversation and sale. If this number is zero every week, something is blocking the transition.
4
Sales made
How many paid clients did you get this week. This is the bottom of your pipeline - and it's always a reflection of everything above it. If sales are low, the problem is always higher up in the numbers.
The golden rule: if you're stuck at any step in the programme, the solution is almost always in the previous step. Low sales? Look at calls. Low calls? Look at conversations. Low conversations? Look at leads. Low leads? Look at your content and visibility habits from Step 5.

Remember: one red week is data. Three red weeks in a row is a pattern that needs addressing. Don't panic at one bad week - do something about three in a row.

If something isn't working, find your situation below and follow the fix. The solution to being stuck at any step is almost always found in the previous step.

RedLow leads - under 5 new people per week
Go back to Step 5 - Daily Visibility Checklist. You're not showing up enough. Check: are you posting 4-5x per week? Are you actively reaching out or just posting and waiting? Are your hooks strong enough to make people stop? The fix is almost always more consistent, more targeted visibility. Check your content prompts and hook swipe file and increase your posting frequency before anything else.
RedLeads but no conversations - people aren't responding
Go back to Step 5 - Fill Your Pipeline. People are seeing you but not engaging. Check: are you posting content that invites a response (polls, questions, calls to respond in comments or DMs)? Are you reaching out to people who engage, or just waiting for them to come to you? Are your DM opening messages personalised or copy-paste? Fix your outreach approach using the Sales Conversation Playbook.
AmberConversations but no calls being booked
Go back to Step 5 - Sales Conversation Playbook. You're talking to people but not moving them forward. Check: are you actually asking for the call, or hoping they'll ask? Are you using the 5-stage DM framework? The transition to a call should be natural and direct - use the exact call transition script from your playbook. Most people are too passive here. You have to ask.
RedCalls happening but no sales closing
Go back to Step 5 - Call and Follow-Up Kit. The call itself is the issue. Check: are you following the discovery call outline? Are you asking enough questions or pitching too early? Are you following up after calls that didn't close? Most sales happen in the follow-up - use your 3-message follow-up sequence. Also check: is your offer clear and specific, or are you overcomplicating it on the call?
AmberEverything looks okay but sales are inconsistent
Focus on volume and consistency. Inconsistent sales usually mean inconsistent activity. Check your weekly numbers over the last 4 weeks - is there a pattern? Weeks you post less tend to be weeks you sell less, with a 2-3 week lag. The fix is showing up consistently even when it feels like nothing is happening. Sales lag behind activity by 2-4 weeks - keep going.
RedNothing is working - no leads, no conversations, no sales
Go back to Step 3 - Find Your People. If you're getting zero traction across the board, the issue is usually one of three things: your niche isn't specific enough, you're not showing up where your audience actually is, or your message isn't resonating. Revisit your Niche Clarity Workbook and Audience Finder Guide. Talk to 3-5 people who fit your ideal client profile and ask them what they're struggling with. The data is always out there - you just need to find it.
