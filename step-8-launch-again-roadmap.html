Go Wider
Grow your audience and reach more of the right people. Same offer, bigger platform.

Going wider means expanding how many people know you exist - through more visibility, new platforms, collaborations, and scaling the lead generation strategies that are already working. You don't change what you sell. You just get it in front of more people.

Month 1
Double down on what's working
  • Identify which content type and platform is generating the most leads - and post more of that
  • Increase posting frequency from 3-4x to 5-7x per week on your primary platform
  • Start repurposing your best content across a second platform (e.g. Instagram → TikTok, or Instagram → LinkedIn)
  • Run a free challenge or workshop to rapidly grow your email list
  • Set a target: 25% increase in weekly leads generated
Month 2
Collaboration and reach
  • Reach out to 5 accounts in complementary niches for collaboration - lives, joint posts, or audience swaps
  • Apply to be a guest on 3 podcasts that serve your ideal client
  • Start a simple referral programme - ask existing clients to refer one person in exchange for a bonus or discount
  • If you haven't started paid ads yet, begin testing with £5-£10/day on your best-performing organic content
Month 3
Systemise and scale
  • Batch create 2-3 weeks of content at once so you can stay consistent even in busy periods
  • Set up a simple affiliate programme - offer 10-20% commission to people who refer paying clients
  • Review your lead generation data and double your budget/effort on the one channel converting best
  • Run a second challenge or free event with the improvements you made from the first

Go Deeper
Develop a more premium offer. Serve fewer people better. Earn more per client.

Going deeper means increasing the value of what you offer and the price you charge for it. Instead of selling to more people, you sell a bigger transformation to fewer people. This path suits you if you want higher income without more volume.

Month 1
Understand what your clients actually need
  • Interview 3-5 existing or past clients - ask what they still struggle with after working with you
  • Identify the next logical problem your clients have after completing your programme
  • Research what a premium version of your offer would look like - more access, more accountability, more done-for-you
  • Raise your current price by 20-30% - test the market before building anything new
Month 2
Design and validate the premium offer
  • Design your premium tier (see the Programme Tiers section) - what's included that your current offer doesn't have?
  • Offer it to 2-3 existing clients at a founding member rate before you formally launch
  • Create a waitlist for your premium offer and build anticipation through your content
  • Document your delivery process so you can serve more people without burning out
Month 3
Launch and position
  • Formally launch your premium offer with a clear sales page or PDF one-pager
  • Position your current offer as the entry point and the premium offer as the next step
  • Create content specifically about the transformation your premium clients experience
  • Aim for 2-3 premium clients in month 3 - that may be more income than 10 standard clients

Go Broader
Build a team or affiliate structure so your business grows beyond your own effort.

Going broader means creating income and reach that isn't solely dependent on your own time and effort. This could mean building an affiliate network, starting a referral programme, or - for network marketing models - growing a team of distributors beneath you.

Setting up a simple affiliate programme
An affiliate programme lets other people refer clients to you in exchange for a commission. It costs you nothing unless you make a sale - and it multiplies your reach without multiplying your workload.
  • Decide your commission rate - 10-20% is standard for coaching and digital products
  • Set up affiliate tracking using Systeme.io (free plan supports affiliates), ThriveCart, or a simple Google Form + manual tracking
  • Recruit your first affiliates from your existing clients - they already believe in what you do
  • Give them everything they need: a unique link, swipe copy, graphics, and email templates
  • Pay out monthly and celebrate their referrals publicly - it motivates them and attracts new affiliates
Month 1
Set up your referral structure
  • Choose your affiliate platform and set it up (Systeme.io free plan is the simplest starting point)
  • Write your affiliate welcome pack - what they're promoting, the commission, how to refer, and all the copy they need
  • Invite your best 3-5 existing clients to become affiliates
  • Create one piece of content about the referral programme to attract more affiliates from your audience
Month 2
Activate and support your affiliates
  • Send your affiliates a monthly content kit - new swipe copy, hooks, and graphics they can use
  • Host a monthly affiliate check-in call - even 20 minutes keeps them engaged and motivated
  • Celebrate every referral publicly (with permission) in your community
  • Track which affiliates are most active and invest more support in them
Month 3
Grow the network
  • Aim for 10+ active affiliates by end of month 3
  • Create an affiliate-only community (WhatsApp group or Facebook group) for peer support
  • Introduce a tiered commission structure - more referrals earns higher commission
  • Start content specifically targeting people who want to be affiliates, not just clients
