Asking for a testimonial feels awkward. It doesn't need to. Here are ready-to-send messages for every situation - copy, personalise, and send.

After completing a programme or package - WhatsApp Hey [name], I've genuinely loved working with you over the past [timeframe]. Watching you go from [where they started] to [where they are now] has been incredible. I'm putting together some case studies and I wondered if you'd be happy to answer a few quick questions about your experience? It'll take about 10 minutes and your answers will help other women in exactly the situation you were in decide if this is right for them. Would that be okay?

After a single session or quick win - WhatsApp Hi [name], so glad our session was useful! I'm building out some client stories and I'd love to include yours if you're open to it. There are just a few questions - totally fine to keep it brief. Would you mind if I sent them over?

Follow-up if they said yes but haven't responded - WhatsApp Hey [name], just following up on the testimonial questions I sent over - no rush at all, I know life gets busy! If it's easier I can jump on a quick 10 minute call and just ask you a few things verbally? Totally your call. Either way, no pressure at all.

Asking to use a DM or comment they already sent you Hey [name], I loved what you said the other day about [their comment]. Would you be happy for me to share that as a testimonial? I'd credit you by first name only unless you'd prefer I keep it anonymous. Let me know and thank you either way - it genuinely means a lot.

Always ask permission before sharing. A quick "is it okay to share this?" takes 10 seconds and protects the relationship. Never share a client's name, photo, or details without explicit consent - and always offer the option to stay anonymous.

A testimonial is only valuable if you actually use it. Here are the formats that convert best and where to put them.

The quote testimonial
Bio, website, sales page
What it is: A 2-4 sentence direct quote from your client in their own words.

What makes it work: Specificity. "Jo helped me make my first £1,500 month while still working full time" is 10x more powerful than "Jo is amazing and changed my life."

Where to use it: Your Instagram bio link page, your website homepage, your sales page, your offer one-pager, as a pinned post.
"Before working with Jo I had three business ideas and zero direction. Six weeks later I had my first paying client and a clear plan for the next three months. I finally feel like this is actually happening." - Sarah, 38
The screenshot testimonial
Stories, posts, sales conversations
What it is: A screenshot of a genuine DM, WhatsApp message, or comment from a client sharing their win.

What makes it work: It's unedited and real. People trust screenshots more than polished quotes because they can see it happened naturally.

Where to use it: Instagram and Facebook stories, as a carousel slide, in your DM conversations when someone asks if it works.
Tip: Keep a dedicated folder on your phone for client win screenshots. When someone sends you a great message, screenshot it immediately before the conversation moves on.
The case study post
Feed posts, email, website
What it is: A longer-form story post that covers before, journey, and after.

What makes it work: It tells a complete story that your ideal client can see themselves in. The "before" is as important as the "after" - it needs to feel familiar.

Where to use it: As a regular feed post (aim for one client story per month), in a nurture email sequence, on your website as a case study page.
Structure: Hook (their before in one line) → Their situation → What changed → The result → Who this is for → CTA
The video testimonial
Sales pages, website, reels
What it is: A short video (60-120 seconds) of your client sharing their experience in their own words.

What makes it work: Authenticity. Seeing and hearing a real person is the closest thing to word of mouth in the digital world.

Where to use it: Your sales page, a pinned reel, your website. Even a slightly shaky phone video works - polish matters far less than genuine emotion.
Ask them to cover: who they are, what they were struggling with, what changed, and one thing they'd say to someone thinking about working with you.
